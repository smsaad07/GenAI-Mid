DELIVERABLE 4 — THE HONESTY SECTION

What the System Gets Wrong, What Is Not Ready for Production, and What It Cost to Run

1. What the System Gets Wrong Today

A. Mid-Month Pro-Rata Wage False Positive (TXN-PAY-004 — 95.83% Precision vs. 100%)

Even with Prompt v3, openai/gpt-oss-120b misclassified TXN-PAY-004 (a direct biscuit line operator hired on 14-Oct-2026 who worked 15/26 days and received PKR 21,346.15 pro-rata on a compliant PKR 37,000/month contract) as Non-Compliant / High Risk. The model saw PKR 21,346 < PKR 37,000 and insisted the monthly floor applied regardless of join date. Until pro-rata daily normalization (paid_base / days_worked) * 26 is computed deterministically before the prompt, HR/IR auditors will have to manually clear mid-month joiners.

B. Unrequested Arithmetic in Cited Figures (TXN-PAY-003 — 99.14% Figure Faithfulness)

In TXN-PAY-003 (19 sanitation workers with PKR 3,500 unlawful PPE deduction), the model accurately cited PKR 37,000, PKR 3,500, and PKR 33,500 in prose, but also emitted 66500.0 (19 × 3,500) in its JSON array, which our strict source-number verifier flagged because 66,500 was not pre-multiplied in the source text.

C. Clean OCR Assumption & Off-Book Collusion Blindspot

Our synthetic dataset supplies clean OCR text for Urdu/English Bilty receipts and weighbridge slips; real thermal/carbon slips at Port Qasim and Hattar have 10–15% handwritten digit noise. Furthermore, cash kickbacks taken outside the factory gate after a compliant bank transfer cannot be detected from ERP/biometric data alone.

2. What We Would Not Yet Put in Front of a Customer

A. Offshore Cloud Inference vs. Phase 1 In-Country Compute Condition

This public demo uses Groq's cloud API on 100% synthetic data. We would never send the Company's real general ledger, contract prices, or payroll to a foreign cloud API. Production deployment requires hosting gpt-oss-20b or Llama-3.3-70B on an in-country/on-premise vLLM server inside the Company's Karachi data center with RBAC per process owner.

B. Groq 8,000 TPM On-Demand Tier Bottleneck

Groq's on-demand tier limits openai/gpt-oss-120b to 8,000 Tokens Per Minute. Because gpt-oss uses internal chain-of-thought tokens before emitting JSON, unbatched bursts hit HTTP 429/413. We mitigated this with 4-item micro-batching and automatic failover to openai/gpt-oss-20b + verified cache fallback.

C. Remaining 8 of 12 Audit Process Areas

Phase 2 proves the architecture on 3 process families (Procure-to-Pay, Payroll/Labour Law, IT SoD). The other 8 areas (Order-to-Cash credit overrides, near-expiry cake write-offs, manual GL journals, BOM yield variances, Capex, Treasury, Tax Annex-C, and Fleet Fuel) are scoped as the next increment.
